# Code of conduct

Super-Herdr contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate
professionally, assume good faith, welcome constructive disagreement, and keep
technical criticism focused on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
publication of another person's credentials or private infrastructure details
are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `mikrodesign@proton.me`. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and may result in warnings, content removal, temporary
restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
